Diagnosis

Symptom is not cause

1Symptom: what the test reported (abort, precondition not reached, failed step)
2Cause: the earliest error finding up to the symptom; an inherited state wins over its later symptoms
3Contributing: other findings, e.g. a failure of its own that does not explain the symptom
4Ruled out: categories a rule checked and found clean
5Verdict: the analyzer can fail a test that CANoe passed

16 rules. Protocol: timeout, missing, unexpected, order, payload, length, rejected. Connection: gap, sequence. State: precondition, disturbed, cascade, abort, manual action. Data: configuration, time alignment.
